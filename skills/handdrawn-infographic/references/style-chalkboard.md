# 粉笔黑板风 (Chalkboard)

深色黑板 + 粉笔手绘：粉笔的颗粒质感、半透明的笔触、可以"擦掉重写"的课堂感。最适合教学和知识科普。

## 视觉特征

- **笔触**：chalk strokes（粉笔笔触）、powdery texture（粉笔粉末颗粒感）、slightly translucent strokes（半透明笔触，能看到底下的黑板）、blended chalk shading（粉笔涂抹渐变）
- **背景**：deep green blackboard（深绿黑板，经典教室色）或 deep charcoal black（深炭黑）
- **细节**：chalk dust on the board（板面上的粉笔灰）、subtle board scratches（隐约板面划痕）、at the bottom a chalk tray（底部粉笔槽）

## 配色方案

- 背景：深绿 `deep green` / 深炭黑 `charcoal black`（二选一，深绿更有教室味）
- 文字/主元素：white chalk（白色粉笔）
- 点缀色：粉笔色系 — 黄 `chalk yellow`、粉 `chalk pink`、蓝 `chalk blue`、橙 `chalk orange`（粉笔色自带柔和的奶感，不是荧光色）

## 英文风格关键词

```
chalk drawing on blackboard, powdery chalk texture, deep green board
background, hand-drawn classroom style, translucent chalk strokes, chalk
dust details, white chalk with pastel chalk accents
```

## 适合 / 不适合

- ✅ 课堂教学、知识科普、培训课件、咖啡店/餐厅黑板菜单风、灵感/格言图、复古校园主题
- ❌ 需要白底印刷的内容、正式商务文档（深色底不好打印）、需要高清小字的内容（粉笔颗粒会让小字发糊）

## 生动化技巧

- **粉笔即道具**：`a piece of chalk resting on the board tray`（粉笔槽里搁着一支粉笔）、`chalk dust falling from the writing`（落下的粉笔灰）— 强化"正在写"的现场感
- **虚化与半擦除**：`a partially erased element fading into chalk dust`（一个擦了一半、正在淡化成粉笔灰的元素）— 黑板的专属叙事手法
- **边框装饰**：`chalk-drawn dashed borders`（粉笔虚线边框）、`dotted underline`（点状下划线）、`hand-drawn stars and arrows in white chalk`
- **发光感**：深色底上白粉笔天然有轻微"发光"感，可以强化：`white chalk lines with a soft glow`（带柔和辉光的白色粉笔线）
- 深色底让亮色更跳：把重点信息用黄色/粉色粉笔标出，视觉焦点自动形成

## 提示词示例片段

```
In chalk drawing style: powdery chalk strokes on a deep green blackboard,
translucent strokes showing the board beneath, chalk dust details and
subtle board scratches. White chalk main elements with chalk yellow and
pink accents for key points. A dashed chalk border around the infographic,
hand-drawn stars and arrows in white chalk. A piece of chalk resting on
the board tray at the bottom. One element partially erased and fading
into chalk dust. White chalk lines with a soft glow.
```
