# 极简线条 line art

单色流畅线条、大量留白、克制点缀的现代手绘风格：一支笔一笔画完的利落感，干净、优雅、高级。最适合需要"设计感"的信息图。

## 视觉特征

- **线条**：single-weight clean lines（统一粗细的干净线条）、elegant continuous strokes（优雅的连续线条）、minimal detail（极简细节）、smooth curves（流畅曲线）
- **关键反差**：线虽少，但信息要准确 — 每个元素用最少的笔数表达清楚，`one-line drawing feel`（一笔画感）
- **留白**：lots of negative space（大量负空间）是本风格的一部分，不是浪费

## 配色方案

- 主色：单色线 — 黑 `black` / 深墨 `deep ink`（首选），可选 深蓝 `deep navy`
- 背景：纯白 / 暖白（`pure white background`）
- 点缀：**至多一个**强调色 — 例如 橙色 `orange`、柠檬黄 `lemon yellow`、克莱因蓝 `klein blue`，只用于最核心的 1-2 个元素或标题
- 进阶：可加极淡的纸纹底色（`very subtle paper tone`）增加质感，但保持整体干净

## 英文风格关键词

```
minimal line art style, single-line drawing, elegant hand-drawn lines,
consistent stroke weight, lots of negative space, pure white background,
one accent color, modern minimalist illustration, clean uncluttered
```

## 适合 / 不适合

- ✅ 科技产品、现代品牌、演示封面、高端内容、极简设计场景、需要"高级感"的科普图
- ❌ 信息量很大的内容（线条风承载不了密集信息，>5 个要点就会乱）、儿童内容、需要热闹氛围的场景

## 生动化技巧

- **一笔画动态**：`a single continuous line forming a bird in flight`（一笔画出一只飞鸟）— 连续线条自带流动感
- **曲线代替直线**：`curved lines for energy, soft wavy lines instead of stiff straight ones`（用曲线表达能量）— 线条风的生动全在曲线的走向
- **一个彩点定焦点**：`the main subject filled in the accent color, everything else pure line`（唯一的主体用强调色填充，其余全部纯线条）— 一抹色就是视觉焦点
- **表情靠线条**：`a small face drawn with three strokes - two dots and a smile curve`（三笔脸：两点一弧）— 极简风的拟人反而更有辨识度
- **场景留白构图**：`a tiny figure standing on a large empty area`（小小的人物站在大片空白里）— 用比例制造故事感和孤独感/高级感

## 提示词示例片段

```
In minimal line art style: single-weight clean lines with a one-line
drawing feel, elegant continuous strokes, consistent stroke weight, pure
white background with lots of negative space. Black ink lines with one
klein blue accent color used only on the main subject. A single continuous
line forming the central icon. The main subject filled in the accent color,
everything else pure line. Small faces drawn with three strokes - two dots
and a smile curve. Curved lines for energy instead of stiff straight ones.
Modern, clean, uncluttered.
```
